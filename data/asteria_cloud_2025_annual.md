# Asteria Cloud Systems — FY2025 Annual Report Summary

## Company Overview
Asteria Cloud Systems develops cloud infrastructure, observability, and AI workflow software for mid-market and enterprise customers. The company sells primarily through annual subscriptions and multi-year enterprise agreements.

## FY2025 Financial Highlights
Asteria reported FY2025 revenue of $1.18 billion, up 24% year over year. Subscription revenue represented 88% of total revenue. Adjusted operating margin improved from 8% in FY2024 to 12% in FY2025.

Gross margin declined slightly from 76% to 74%. Management attributed the decline to higher GPU infrastructure costs associated with the rollout of Asteria Intelligence, its generative AI product suite.

Free cash flow reached $168 million compared with $112 million in the prior year.

## Customer Metrics
The company ended FY2025 with 4,980 customers generating more than $100,000 in annual recurring revenue. Net revenue retention was 119%, down from 124% one year earlier.

Management stated that the decline in net revenue retention reflected slower seat expansion among technology customers and pricing pressure in smaller accounts.

## Strategic Priorities for 2026
1. Expand Asteria Intelligence across the existing enterprise customer base.
2. Reduce GPU inference costs through model routing and workload optimization.
3. Increase cross-selling between observability and AI workflow products.
4. Improve sales productivity in Europe.

## Risk Factors
- Rising GPU and cloud infrastructure expenses.
- Longer enterprise procurement cycles.
- Aggressive pricing from larger cloud platform competitors.
- Customer concentration among technology and digital-native companies.
- Regulatory uncertainty around enterprise AI use.

## 2026 Outlook
For FY2026, management initially expected revenue growth between 20% and 22% and adjusted operating margin between 13% and 14%.

Management said growth would likely be stronger in the second half of 2026 if AI product adoption accelerated.
