# Asteria Cloud Systems — Q1 2026 Earnings Report

## Financial Results
Q1 2026 revenue was $306 million, representing 21% year-over-year growth. Subscription revenue grew 23%.

Gross margin was 72%, down from 74% for FY2025. Management said the decline was primarily caused by GPU inference costs from increased usage of Asteria Intelligence.

Adjusted operating margin was 12.5%.

## Customer Trends
Net revenue retention declined to 116%. Management cited slower expansion among large software customers and more cautious IT budgets in Europe.

Enterprise customers continued to adopt Asteria Intelligence, but most deployments remained in pilot or limited-production phases.

## Management Commentary
The CFO stated that the company expected infrastructure cost pressure to remain elevated through the first half of 2026.

The CEO emphasized that AI-related bookings were strong, but revenue recognition would lag bookings because customers were starting with smaller production workloads.

## Risks
- Elevated GPU inference costs.
- Longer procurement and security-review cycles.
- Weakness in European technology spending.
- Potential margin pressure if AI usage grows faster than pricing improvements.

## Guidance
Asteria maintained FY2026 revenue growth guidance of 20% to 22%.

However, management revised the expected timing of growth and said Q2 growth could fall below 20% before accelerating in the second half.

The company maintained its 13% to 14% adjusted operating margin target for the full year.
