# Asteria Cloud Systems — Q2 2026 Earnings Report

## Financial Results
Q2 2026 revenue was $319 million, up 17% year over year. This represented a slowdown from 21% growth in Q1.

Subscription revenue increased 19%.

Gross margin improved slightly to 73% from 72% in Q1 after the company introduced workload routing and renegotiated several cloud infrastructure contracts.

Adjusted operating margin was 13.1%.

## Drivers of the Growth Slowdown
Management attributed slower revenue growth to three main factors:
1. Longer enterprise procurement cycles, especially in Europe.
2. Slower expansion from existing technology-sector customers.
3. Delayed conversion of AI pilots into large production contracts.

The CFO said new logo activity remained healthy and that the slowdown was concentrated in expansion revenue.

## AI Product Adoption
Asteria Intelligence bookings grew 48% year over year. However, management said many customers were still moving through security and governance reviews.

Average production deployment size increased during the quarter, but not fast enough to offset slower seat expansion in the core observability product.

## Updated Risks
Management continued to highlight procurement delays and technology-sector weakness.

It added two risks:
- Enterprise AI regulation could delay production deployments.
- Competitors were beginning to bundle AI observability features into broader cloud contracts at discounted prices.

GPU cost pressure was described as less severe than in Q1 due to routing improvements.

## Updated Guidance
Asteria reduced FY2026 revenue growth guidance from 20%–22% to 17%–19%.

The company kept adjusted operating margin guidance at 13%–14%.

Management stated that the second-half acceleration thesis remained possible, but visibility was lower than at the start of the year.
