# Analyst Risk Notes — Asteria Cloud Systems

## Persistent Risks
Across the FY2025 annual report and the first two quarters of 2026, three risks recur consistently:
- Longer enterprise procurement cycles.
- Slower expansion among technology-sector customers.
- Competitive pricing pressure from major cloud platforms.

## Changing Risks
GPU infrastructure cost was one of the most important risks in FY2025 and Q1 2026. By Q2 2026, this risk had moderated because workload routing and contract renegotiation improved gross margin.

Regulatory uncertainty became more prominent in Q2 as customers increased scrutiny around AI governance and data handling.

## Key Interpretation
Asteria's central challenge is not a collapse in new customer demand. New logo activity remains healthy. The more significant issue is slower conversion from pilot to production and weaker expansion revenue from existing customers.

This distinction matters because it suggests the slowdown is primarily related to monetization timing and customer expansion rather than broad customer loss.
