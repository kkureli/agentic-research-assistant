# Asteria Cloud Systems — 2026 Strategy Memo

## Objective
Asteria's 2026 operating plan is designed to protect long-term AI growth while improving unit economics.

## Priority 1: Convert AI Pilots to Production
The company has a large pipeline of customers testing Asteria Intelligence. The primary bottlenecks are security review, governance approval, data residency, and cost predictability.

Product teams are building deployment templates for regulated industries to shorten approval cycles.

## Priority 2: Improve AI Gross Margin
The infrastructure team is implementing model routing, caching, prompt compression, and reserved GPU capacity.

The internal target is to reduce average inference cost per AI workflow by at least 25% by the end of 2026.

## Priority 3: Rebalance Geographic Exposure
Europe underperformed North America during the first half of 2026.

Asteria plans to shift sales investment toward financial services, healthcare, and industrial customers where demand has been more resilient.

## Priority 4: Defend Against Platform Bundling
Large cloud providers increasingly bundle observability and AI monitoring features into infrastructure contracts.

Asteria's response is to emphasize cross-cloud neutrality, deeper workflow automation, and independent governance controls.

## Key Execution Risks
- Enterprise customers may remain in pilot mode longer than planned.
- Cost reductions may be slower if GPU prices remain high.
- Sales teams may struggle to reposition from observability-only deals toward AI platform deals.
- Platform vendors may use discounting to limit Asteria's pricing power.
