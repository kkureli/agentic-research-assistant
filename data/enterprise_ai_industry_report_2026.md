# 2026 Enterprise AI Infrastructure Industry Report

## Market Growth
Enterprise adoption of generative AI continues to expand, but production deployment is progressing more slowly than pilot activity.

Many companies report strong experimentation levels but longer timelines for security, governance, and procurement approval.

## Key Industry Constraints

### Governance
Enterprises increasingly require model evaluation, auditability, data residency controls, and usage monitoring before moving AI workloads into production.

### Infrastructure Cost
GPU and inference costs remain a major constraint, although model routing, caching, smaller specialized models, and reserved capacity are improving unit economics.

### Vendor Bundling
Large cloud platforms are bundling AI monitoring, security, and observability into broader contracts.

Independent vendors must demonstrate cross-cloud support, governance depth, or superior workflow automation.

### Procurement Cycles
Procurement cycles for AI software are often longer than for traditional SaaS because security, legal, compliance, and data teams are involved.

## Outlook
The report expects AI software revenue growth to remain strong, but warns that vendors may experience a gap between bookings growth and recognized revenue when customers stay in pilot mode for extended periods.

Vendors with strong gross-margin discipline and clear production deployment pathways are expected to outperform.
