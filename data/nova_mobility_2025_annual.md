# Nova Mobility — FY2025 Annual Report Summary

## Company Overview
Nova Mobility provides fleet optimization software, routing systems, and autonomous vehicle operations tools for logistics and delivery companies.

## FY2025 Financial Highlights
FY2025 revenue was $842 million, up 18% year over year.

Software subscription revenue grew 26%, while lower-margin hardware and installation revenue grew 6%.

Gross margin increased to 61% from 58% as software became a larger part of the revenue mix.

Adjusted operating margin was 9%.

## Customer Base
Nova served 1,420 enterprise customers at year-end. The top 20 customers represented 31% of revenue.

Customer retention remained above 95%, but management noted that logistics customers were becoming more cautious about large hardware deployments.

## 2026 Strategy
1. Software-led fleet optimization.
2. International expansion in the Middle East.
3. Reduced dependence on custom hardware installations.
4. Higher recurring revenue contribution.

## Risk Factors
- Customer concentration.
- Delays in fleet hardware deployments.
- Exposure to logistics industry capital spending.
- Regulatory uncertainty for autonomous operations.
- Component supply disruptions.

## FY2026 Outlook
Management guided for 15% to 17% revenue growth and gross margin between 62% and 64%.
