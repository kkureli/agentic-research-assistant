# Nova Mobility — Q1 2026 Earnings Report

## Financial Results
Q1 revenue was $215 million, up 16% year over year.

Software subscription revenue grew 28%, while hardware and deployment revenue increased only 3%.

Gross margin reached 63%.

Adjusted operating margin was 10%.

## Demand Trends
North American logistics customers delayed several hardware refresh projects because of tighter capital budgets.

Software demand remained healthy, particularly for routing optimization and fleet analytics.

## International Expansion
Nova signed two large customers in the Gulf region and opened a regional support center.

Management said Middle East revenue would remain modest in the first half but could contribute more meaningfully in Q4.

## Risks
- Continued weakness in logistics capital spending.
- Delays in hardware projects.
- Customer concentration.
- Integration risk from rapid international expansion.

## Guidance
Nova maintained FY2026 revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17%.

Gross margin guidance remained 62% to 64%.
