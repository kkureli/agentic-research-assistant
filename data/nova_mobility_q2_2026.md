# Nova Mobility — Q2 2026 Earnings Report

## Financial Results
Q2 revenue was $224 million, up 14% year over year.

Software subscription revenue grew 25%.

Hardware and deployment revenue declined 2% as several customers delayed fleet modernization projects.

Gross margin rose to 64%.

Adjusted operating margin was 11.2%.

## Revenue Growth Slowdown
Growth slowed from 16% in Q1 to 14% in Q2.

Management attributed the slowdown mainly to hardware deployment delays and cautious logistics-sector capital spending.

Software demand remained comparatively resilient.

## Middle East Expansion
The company reported early success with two regional logistics operators. Management said the Middle East business was tracking slightly ahead of its internal plan.

## Updated Risks
- Capital spending weakness among logistics customers.
- Customer concentration.
- Project execution risk in international markets.
- Autonomous vehicle regulation.
- Hardware supply timing.

## Guidance
Nova reduced FY2026 revenue growth guidance from 15%–17% to 13%–15%.

Gross margin guidance increased from 62%–64% to 63%–65% because of the stronger software mix.
