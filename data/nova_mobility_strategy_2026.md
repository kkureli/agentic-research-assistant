# Nova Mobility — 2026 Strategy Memo

## Strategic Goal
Nova is shifting from hardware-heavy fleet deployments toward higher-margin recurring software.

## Software Mix Expansion
Management aims to increase software subscription revenue to more than 60% of total revenue by the end of 2027.

The company is investing in AI-assisted routing, predictive maintenance, and fleet utilization analytics.

## International Growth
The Middle East is the highest-priority expansion region for 2026.

Nova plans to use local implementation partners rather than building a large internal deployment workforce.

## Margin Improvement
The strategic plan assumes software growth continues to outpace hardware growth.

This mix shift is expected to improve gross margin even if total revenue growth slows temporarily.

## Key Execution Risks
- Hardware delays may reduce near-term revenue faster than software can compensate.
- Local partners may create implementation-quality variability.
- Large customers may demand bundled pricing.
- International regulation may slow autonomous operations products.
