# Enterprise Software Market Summary — Q2 2026

## Spending Environment
Enterprise software budgets remained positive but uneven during Q2 2026.

North American spending was more resilient than European spending. Technology-sector customers were among the most cautious buyers.

## Buying Behavior
Customers increasingly preferred smaller initial contracts with expansion tied to measurable production usage.

Security reviews and procurement approvals lengthened for AI-related software.

## Pricing
Large cloud platforms used bundled pricing aggressively.

Independent software vendors with narrow feature differentiation faced pressure to discount.

## AI Monetization
Bookings growth for AI products remained strong across the sector, but revenue recognition often lagged due to pilot-heavy adoption.

## Margin Trends
Vendors with high inference costs focused on model routing, caching, workload scheduling, and negotiated cloud commitments.

These optimizations produced visible gross-margin improvement at several companies during Q2.
